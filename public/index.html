A city that fits you.

Pick a few things you love — a musician, a film, a restaurant. Adjacent builds a day in a city you have not visited, and shows the taste evidence behind every stop.

Add two or three. The more specific, the stranger and better the day gets.
How the evidence works. Every stop carries the affinity score Qloo returned for your seeds, plus the tags that describe the audience behind it. When Qloo returns a per-seed explanation it is shown as a chip; on the hackathon key it usually does not, so the chip reads "no explanation returned" rather than inventing a story. Empty slots are named, not filled. The cross-media panel asks the same seeds for artists, films and books; a domain Qloo cannot answer is shown as an error, not as a guess.

Agents can use this. The same taste logic is exposed over MCP at POST /mcp with three tools: find_entities, plan_day and cross_media. The web page is just one client.
